# Sovereign Awareness Platform — Core Vision & Philosophy

## Foundational Principle: Total Situational Omniscience
God's Eye View exists to provide individuals with an uncompromised, sovereign picture of the physical world. By aggregating public, commercial, and personal telemetry into a unified 3D coordinate system, the platform transforms disconnected signals into actionable, spatial understanding.

## Core Pillars

### 1. Zero Cloud Dependency & Local-First Sovereignty
- All spatial computations, dead reckoning, interpolation, and rendering occur locally on the operator's machine or edge device.
- Sensitive credentials, telemetry history, and private recordings never touch foreign servers without explicit operator consent.
- The platform remains operational in degraded or air-gapped network conditions via local caching, offline vector tiles, and bundled infrastructure layers.

### 2. High-Fidelity Multi-Domain Sensor Fusion
- **Orbital**: Satellite ephemerides propagated in real-time via SGP4 with true GMST alignment.
- **Atmospheric**: Comprehensive live air picture merging commercial ADS-B transponders, military feeds, emergency squawks, and meteorological fields.
- **Maritime**: Terrestrial and satellite AIS feeds mapping commercial cargo, passenger vessels, and tactical shipping.
- **Terrestrial & Urban**: Real-time traffic flow dynamics, public CCTV projection frustums, high-resolution street panoramas, and civic transit trackers.
- **Physical Personal Sensing**: Ingestion of wearable biometrics, 360° visual camera spheres, wearable LiDAR, and RF spectrum monitors.

### 3. Tactile & Cognitive Immersion
- **Cockpit & First-Person Ride**: Seamless transition from planetary bird's-eye view to cockpit-locked ride-along over photorealistic 3D terrain.
- **Sensor Optics (Sensor Modes)**: Dynamic GLSL sensor shaders simulating CRT tactical monitors, Night Vision (NVG), thermal imaging (Ironbow/White-Hot FLIR), and high-contrast reconnaissance modes.
- **Conversational & Voice HUD**: Hands-free voice interface delivering concise, five-word tactical summaries and spatial entity querying.

### 4. Ethical Boundaries
- God's Eye View models systems, assets, natural phenomena, and public infrastructure.
- The platform strictly rejects person-tracking, facial recognition databases, and privacy-violating individual surveillance tools. People are not a query type here.
